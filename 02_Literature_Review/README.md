# Literature Review

## Overview

This folder contains the literature review for the PediVision AI research project.

The purpose of the review is to examine existing research on pediatric brain MRI analysis, deep learning, multimodal learning, clinical data integration, missing-modality robustness, and explainable AI.

## Objectives

* Identify relevant published research.
* Understand the datasets and methods used in existing studies.
* Compare reported evaluation metrics and findings.
* Identify limitations and potential research opportunities.
* Verify the proposed research gap before claiming novelty.

## Research Topics

1. Pediatric brain MRI classification.
2. Deep learning for medical image analysis.
3. Multimodal MRI and clinical data fusion.
4. Missing-modality robustness.
5. Explainable AI for medical imaging.
6. Pediatric brain tumor prediction and classification.

## Literature Matrix

The file `literature_matrix.csv` will be used to record and compare relevant research papers.
