
# Paper 03 Summary

## Paper Title
A multi-institutional pediatric dataset of clinical radiology MRIs by the Children’s Brain Tumor Network

## Publication Details
- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** Scientific Data
- **Source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10723526/

## Research Objective
To describe a large, multi-institutional pediatric brain tumor MRI dataset and its associated clinical and biological information.

## Dataset
- MRI examinations: 23,101
- Patients: 1,526
- Data source: Children's Brain Tumor Network (CBTN)

## Data Types
- Clinical radiology MRI scans
- Longitudinal imaging data
- Clinical information
- Digital pathology data
- Molecular and genomic data

## Main Contribution
The study describes a large pediatric brain tumor dataset that can support research in medical imaging, clinical data analysis, and healthcare AI.

## Relevance to PediVision
This paper is relevant because it describes a pediatric brain tumor dataset with MRI and associated clinical information.

The dataset may be useful for exploring multimodal AI research, subject to access permissions, data availability, and the presence of suitable variables and labels.

## Limitations
A detailed review of the full paper is needed to identify specific limitations relevant to PediVision.

## Possible Research Opportunities
- Investigate which clinical variables are available alongside MRI scans.
- Explore whether MRI and clinical information can be linked at the patient level.
- Assess whether the available data support MRI-only and multimodal classification experiments.

These are potential directions, not confirmed research gaps.
