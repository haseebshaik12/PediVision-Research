
# Paper 02 Summary

## Paper Title
Deep learning based clinico-radiological model for paediatric brain tumor detection and subtype prediction

## Publication Details
- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** Exploration of Targeted Anti-tumor Therapy
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.37349/etat.2023.00159

## Research Objective
To investigate pediatric brain tumor MRI features and develop a deep-learning-based approach for tumor detection, segmentation, and subtype prediction.

## Dataset
- Total cases: 94
- Tumor cases: 75
- Normal MRI cases: 19

## Tumor Types Mentioned
- Ependymoma
- Medulloblastoma
- Brainstem glioma
- Pilocytic astrocytoma

## Main Approach
The study used MRI features and deep learning to explore tumor segmentation and classification.

## Evaluation Metrics
- Dice score
- Hausdorff95 distance
- Sensitivity
- Specificity
- Predictive values
- Accuracy
- Cohen's kappa

## Main Findings
The paper reports promising results for MRI-based abnormality detection and tumor subtype prediction. The reported findings require careful interpretation because the dataset was relatively small.

## Limitations
A detailed review of the full methodology is still needed to confirm the study's limitations, including data splitting, external validation, and generalizability.

## Relevance to PediVision
This study is relevant to pediatric brain tumor analysis using MRI and deep learning.

PediVision may investigate whether combining MRI-derived features with structured clinical information improves classification compared with MRI-only and clinical-only models.

## Possible Research Opportunities
- Compare MRI-only, clinical-only, and multimodal models.
- Investigate the availability of clinical variables in the selected dataset.
- Evaluate generalizability and robustness if suitable data are available.

These are potential research directions, not confirmed research gaps.
